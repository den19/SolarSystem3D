Solar System 3D is an interactive 3D solar-system simulator and planetarium for your phone. Watch planets orbit the Sun, explore moons and comets, change scale and time speed — great for school, self-study, and anyone curious about astronomy and space.

NEW: PROBE MODE
Launch educational flights of legendary spacecraft inside the solar-system model. Pick a start body, set thrust and heading, follow the green trajectory and live telemetry — speed, distance, nearest bodies. View the flight in World, Chase, and Cockpit cameras. The catalog includes historical models such as Luna-1, Mars-3, Voyager, New Horizons, Juno, and other missions from different countries, each with a short info card.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• 8 planets of the Solar System and the Sun in a 3D model
• Major moons: Moon, Phobos and Deimos, Galilean moons of Jupiter, Titan, Triton
• 10 periodic comets with orbits and changing appearance
• Probe mode: launch, aiming, telemetry, chase and cockpit cameras
• Real orbits with eccentricity and inclination
• Educational layout and a mode closer to real distance scale
• Time controls: pause and speed from 0.25× to 256×
• Orbit lines, body labels, minimap, and gravity grid
• Short info cards for planets, moons, comets, and probes
• Free camera look: gestures, zoom, focus on a selected body
• Clean-screen mode without extra UI

WHO IT'S FOR
• Students — a clear solar-system model and spaceflight history
• Teachers — demos of orbits, moons, comets, and probe paths
• Astronomy fans — explore without a telescope

HOW TO USE
Open the simulation, pick a planet, moon, or comet, read its description, and tune orbits, scale, and time speed. To try Probe mode, enable it on the side panel, choose a start body and spacecraft model, adjust Thrust and Heading, then tap Launch. Gestures let you look around freely.
